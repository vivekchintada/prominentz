# Module 2 — Workforce completion

## Goal
Complete the workforce MVP for real scheduling and payroll preparation.

## Implement
- Drag-and-drop shift reassignment and date movement with keyboard-accessible alternatives.
- Copy day/week and reusable shift templates.
- Open-shift claiming and complete peer/manager swap approvals.
- Schedule publication versions and change notifications.
- WhatsApp/email notifications behind configured providers.
- Demand forecast using historical hourly sales, orders, reservations, and day-of-week.
- Coverage rules by role/station and understaffing warnings.
- Editable timesheets with mandatory manager reason and audit trail.
- Overtime rules configurable per restaurant; payroll-provider adapters after generic CSV.

## Acceptance
No overlapping shifts; approved leave and availability warnings; published changes are visible to staff; actual hours reconcile to attendance; every manual adjustment is auditable; CSV totals match approved timesheets.
