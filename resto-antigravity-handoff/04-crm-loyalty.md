# Module 4 — CRM and loyalty

## Goal
Convert ordering/POS activity into repeat-customer growth.

## Implement
- Unified customer profile from POS, QR, online ordering, phone, and delivery sources.
- Customer consent, communication preferences, tags, allergies, notes, visit/spend metrics.
- Configurable points earning, tiers, rewards, expiry, and manual adjustments.
- Coupon campaigns with channel/date/category/minimum/usage restrictions.
- Reward redemption in POS and online checkout with transactional balance protection.
- Customer segmentation: new, repeat, high-value, lapsed, birthday, dietary tags.
- Campaign drafts and provider adapters for email/SMS/WhatsApp.
- Customer timeline and loyalty liability reporting.

## Acceptance
No duplicate customer profiles for normalized phone/email; points are awarded once per completed payment; refunds reverse points; redemptions are atomic; consent is enforced before messaging.
